# Context Template

```markdown
# {Context Name}

{One or two sentences defining the context's responsibility and boundary.}

## Language

**{Canonical Term}** ({cardinality or ownership when useful}):
{Definition in one or two sentences.}
Avoid: {conflicting synonyms}

## Flagged Ambiguities

{Unresolved term or boundary}: {conflict and the decision still needed}
```

- Omit `Avoid` when no conflicting synonym exists.
- Omit `Flagged Ambiguities` when none remain.
- Keep requirements, implementation guidance, and decisions out of the glossary.
