# Sharing the Inbox Grant on Acceptance

The asked side shares its inbox grant only when it accepts, so a requester it turns down never holds its delivery capability. The grant therefore also accepts the contact when it arrives on the requester's session, as a contact acceptance does. A requester whose restore lost the acceptance then becomes accepted again once the asked side's grant reaches its new session.

## Alternatives

- Share the grant with the asked side's first reply: a requester it then turned down would keep delivering until it rotated its inbox.
- Leave a lost acceptance to recovery: recovery resends only unacknowledged messages, so an acceptance the requester acknowledged before restoring an older backup never comes back.
